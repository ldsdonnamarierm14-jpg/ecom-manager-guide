Amazon Playbook

The most complex channel in your portfolio — deserves 40–50% of your total management time.

Amazon combines the highest sales volume with the most complex ecosystem: A9/A10 algorithm, PPC, FBA logistics, strict compliance, and constant listing suppression risks.
Listing content — click each to expand
  • Formula: Brand + Main Keyword + Key Feature + Size/Quantity/Variant
  • Keep under 200 characters — mobile shows ~80 chars, so lead with the most important info
  • Capitalize major words; no ALL-CAPS, no symbols like !, $, or *
  • Front-load primary search keyword naturally — no keyword stuffing
  • Example: "BrandName 50-lb Premium Dog Food — High Protein, Grain-Free, Adult Breeds, 30 lb Bag"
  • Each bullet = one benefit, starting with a bold ALL-CAPS label (e.g., EASY INSTALLATION –)
  • Lead with the benefit, not the feature: "Reduces energy bills by 30%" not "Has insulation"
  • Address common customer questions/objections in bullets 3–5
  • Include secondary keywords naturally without stuffing
  • Keep each bullet under 200 characters — mobile truncates long bullets
  • Prioritize A+ for your top 20% ASINs by revenue first — highest ROI
  • Use comparison charts to cross-sell SKUs and explain variants
  • Lifestyle imagery + feature callouts outperform pure product shots
  • Brand Stories module: tell your brand narrative, build trust and loyalty
  • A++ (Premium A+): reserved for top sellers — includes video, hotspot images, Q&A
  • Avoid: keyword stuffing, blurry images, generic copy that fits any brand
  • Image 1 (Hero): White background, product only, fills 85% of frame — no lifestyle
  • Image 2: Key features callout/infographic with dimensions or components labeled
  • Image 3: In-use lifestyle shot — product in real environment, aspirational
  • Image 4: Close-up of differentiating feature (material, mechanism, finish)
  • Image 5: Comparison chart vs competitor or older model
  • Image 6: Packaging, certifications, or "what's in the box"
  • Image 7: Social proof / review highlight or usage instruction graphic
  • Minimum 1500×1500px; JPEG preferred; upload 2000px+ to enable customer zoom
  • 250 bytes in Search Terms field — use synonyms, alternate spellings, long-tail terms
  • Do NOT repeat words already in your title — Amazon already indexes those
  • Research tools: Helium 10, Jungle Scout, DataDive, or Amazon Brand Analytics
  • Subject Matter fields (up to 5×50 chars): competitor conquesting terms go here
  • Refresh keywords quarterly using Brand Analytics search frequency data
Amazon advertising (PPC) — 4 campaign types

🎯 Sponsored Products

Most impactful type. Target high-intent keywords and competitor ASINs. Run auto campaigns to harvest new keywords, manual campaigns to scale winners. Review weekly.

🏆 Sponsored Brands

Top-of-search headline + video ads. Drive to your Brand Store or best-seller ASIN. Excellent for new product launches and brand awareness campaigns.

📍 Sponsored Display

Retargeting and product detail page placements. Reach shoppers who viewed your product but didn't buy. Also targets competitor product pages effectively.

📡 DSP (Demand Side Platform)

Programmatic off-Amazon advertising. Best for brands spending $50K+/month. Ideal for new launches and seasonal campaigns with larger budgets.

Channel playbook

Walmart

Mass retail — 2nd largest US e-commerce channel. Less competition than Amazon in most categories with lower PPC CPCs.

📊 Listing Quality Score (LQS)

Walmart's content score — target 80+. Fill every attribute: title, description, 4+ images, complete specs, and all category-specific fields.

🛒 Pro Seller Badge

Equivalent to Amazon's Buy Box. Achieved by maintaining strong seller metrics: on-time delivery, low cancel rate, valid tracking, and high ratings.

📦 Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS)

Enroll top SKUs in WFS to boost Buy Box eligibility and unlock Walmart+ 2-day shipping badge — a major conversion driver.

💰 Walmart Connect (PPC)

Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands mirror Amazon's structure. Lower CPCs than Amazon — a major opportunity in competitive categories.

⚠️ Price Parity Rule

Walmart enforces strict price parity — if your Amazon price is lower, Walmart will automatically unpublish your listing. Monitor pricing daily across channels.

🎯 Savings Spotlight & Rollback

Work with your Walmart buyer to participate in Rollback events and Savings Spotlight placements — these drive significant incremental traffic.

Key compliance KPIs
  • ✓ On-Time Delivery Rate: target 95%+
  • ✓ Valid Tracking Rate: target 99%+
  • ✓ Cancellation Rate: keep below 2%
  • ✓ GMV (Gross Merchandise Value) — primary revenue metric
  • ✓ LQS score per item — fix anything below 70 immediately
Channel playbook

Chewy

Pet specialty retailer with an intensely loyal customer base. Primarily a wholesale/vendor relationship — some brands also sell on Chewy Marketplace (3P).

🐾 Required Pet Attributes

Species, life stage, breed size, flavor, weight, ingredient list, guaranteed analysis, and nutritional information are all mandatory fields — missing any will suppress your listing.

📸 Emotional Photography

Lifestyle shots with pets dramatically outperform studio shots on Chewy. Customers are highly emotionally engaged — invest in warm, aspirational imagery with animals.

🔄 Autoship (Subscribe & Save)

Chewy's biggest differentiator. Getting products enrolled in Autoship creates recurring revenue and significantly higher customer LTV. This is your #1 strategic priority.

🤝 Account Manager Relationship

Chewy has dedicated vendor account reps. Build that relationship — it's the path to Chewy Picks placements, homepage features, and promotional email inclusions.

⭐ Reviews Strategy

Chewy reviews are among the most trusted in retail. Encourage post-purchase review requests. Respond to every negative review within 48 hours — Chewy monitors this.

📅 Promotional Calendar

Chewy runs aggressive promos on Cyber Monday, holiday season, and category awareness months (National Pet Month in May). Submit deal nominations 6 weeks ahead.

Channel playbook

Lowe's

Home improvement wholesale vendor. Omnichannel presence (in-store + online) with strict OTIF compliance requirements.

🔩 Technical Specs are #1

Dimensions, materials, compatibility lists, installation requirements, certifications, and warranty info. Contractors and DIYers rely on specs to make purchase decisions — incomplete specs = lost sales.

📐 Photography Requirements

In-context installation/use shots alongside clean white-background images. Include dimensions overlay graphics and "what's in the box" imagery. Show the product installed.

📋 OTIF Compliance

On-Time In-Full is strictly enforced with a structured chargeback schedule. Use Lowe's routing guide precisely. Late or short shipments will trigger automatic deductions.

🏗️ Lowe's Pro Segment

Growing B2B channel serving contractors and property managers. If your products serve pros, request Pro category placement and B2B pricing tiers from your buyer.

🌸 Spring Black Friday

March–April is Lowe's biggest promotional event. Outdoor, garden, and home improvement categories spike. Submit deal nominations to your buyer 8+ weeks ahead.

🔌 Vendor Portal Management

Use Lowe's Vendor Portal for PO management, item setup, and content updates. Respond to POs and confirmations within required SLA windows to avoid compliance flags.

Channel playbook

Tractor Supply Co. (TSC)

Farm, ranch, and rural lifestyle retailer. Customers value durability, practicality, and products built for real work.

👨‍🌾 Know Your Customer

Rural homeowners, hobby farmers, ranchers, and equestrian customers. They're practical, value durability, and are skeptical of marketing fluff. Speak their language.

✍️ Copy Tone

Be direct and practical. Lead with durability, weather resistance, compatibility with equipment, and ease of maintenance. American-made claims resonate strongly with this audience.

📸 Real-World Photography

Farm, barn, and outdoor settings dramatically outperform studio shots. Show products being used by farmers or ranchers. Include livestock/animals where relevant and authentic.

🌱 Key Category Seasons

Spring planting, summer farm maintenance, fall harvest prep, and winter animal care are the four major seasonal themes. Build promotional submissions around these cycles.

🐄 Core Product Categories

Animal feed & supplements, fencing, power tools, outdoor power equipment, workwear, large-breed pet products, and livestock care supplies are TSC's highest-traffic categories.

📡 EDI Compliance

TSC requires EDI 850/856/810 transactions. Use their supplier portal for item setup. Review routing guide compliance carefully — freight terms (collect vs prepaid) vary by order type.

Channel playbook

Wayfair

Home goods, furniture, and décor — primarily a dropship model. Customers shop by room inspiration, not just product specs.

🏠 Room Scene Images Are Essential

Wayfair customers shop by inspiration — room scenes are not optional, they're the primary selling tool. Multiple angles required. Dimensions callout images are also mandatory.

📦 CastleGate Fulfillment

Wayfair's fulfillment network dramatically improves search visibility and conversion rates. Enroll your top SKUs in CastleGate for maximum algorithmic placement advantage.

💲 Competitive Pricing Algorithm

Wayfair's algorithm monitors pricing against competitors. Products priced significantly above comparable items lose search visibility. Monitor and adjust pricing regularly.

📉 Return Rate Management

Returns are the biggest profitability risk on Wayfair — especially for furniture. Reduce them with accurate dimensions, clear assembly instructions, and realistic room scene images.

🎉 Way Day — Annual Sale Event

Wayfair's equivalent of Prime Day, typically held in April. Submit products and promotional pricing to Wayfair Extranet 6 weeks ahead. This is your biggest Wayfair revenue opportunity.

⚡ Dropship SLA

Ship within 1 business day for direct-ship orders. Supplier Performance Score is tied to this — drops in score reduce your algorithmic visibility across all your SKUs on the platform.

Channel playbook

Home Depot

The largest US DIY home improvement retailer. Wholesale vendor or Pro Xtra Marketplace seller with omnichannel (in-store + online) presence.

🔧 Technical Completeness

Compatibility details, exact dimensions, certifications (UL, CSA, Energy Star, ADA), installation specs, and warranty terms are all critical. Incomplete specs directly reduce search ranking.

📸 Application Photography

Show products installed or in-use — not just the product itself. Group compatible items together. Include spec sheet graphics as images. Show scale with context.

👷 Pro Xtra Channel

Home Depot Pro serves contractors and property managers at significant volume. If you sell contractor-grade or jobsite products, request Pro category placement and volume pricing.

📋 98%+ OTIF Target

The strictest OTIF requirements in your channel mix. Coordinate closely with logistics — routing guides and delivery appointment scheduling are strictly enforced with chargebacks.

🌷 Spring Black Friday

Home Depot's biggest annual promotional event (late March–April). Submit deal nominations to your merchant contact 8–10 weeks in advance. Outdoor and garden categories peak here.

🖥️ Partner Portal Management

Home Depot's supplier portal (HDUS) handles PO management, item setup, and content updates. Respond to all PO events within SLA windows. Check for content score alerts weekly.

Content syndication — use a PIM tool

Why you need a PIM (Product Information Manager)

Tools like Salsify, Akeneo, or Syndigo let you manage one master content record and push channel-specific versions to all 7 retailers automatically. Manual updates across 7 channels do not scale — a PIM is a necessity, not a luxury.

Channel-specific content rules
  • No HTML in bullet points or description (Amazon strips it)
  • Strict image policy: white background hero, no promotional text on main image
  • A+ Content requires Brand Registry enrollment
  • 200-char title limit; 250-byte backend keyword field
  • Requires GTIN/UPC for all items — no exceptions
  • 1000-character long description field; HTML allowed
  • Uses proprietary Listing Quality Score (LQS) — target 80+
  • 4+ images required for full content score; 6+ recommended
  • Pet-specific attributes mandatory: life stage, breed size, species, flavor
  • Ingredient list and guaranteed analysis required for consumables

  • Technical specs are the highest priority: exact dimensions, materials, certifications
  • Compatibility information must be complete and accurate — returns spike without it
  • TSC: stress durability, weather resistance, and practical farm/outdoor use in copy tone
  • HD/Lowe's: include installation requirements and warranty information in description
  • Room scene lifestyle images are required — product-only shots significantly underperform
  • Multiple angles required: front, back, side, detail, and in-room

Sales & Promotions

How to drive revenue growth through pricing strategy, advertising, and promotional planning across all channels.

Pricing strategy fundamentals

📊 MAP Policy — enforce it

Establish a Minimum Advertised Price policy and enforce it across all sellers. MAP violations on Amazon trigger automatic suppression on other channels (Walmart price parity check). This is table stakes for channel health.

🔄 Repricing tools (Amazon)

Tools like Informed.co or BQool automate Buy Box price optimization in real-time. Set floors (MAP) and ceilings (target margin). Manual repricing on Amazon is not viable at scale.

Promotional tools by channel
  • Lightning Deals: 4–12 hour flash deals on the Deals page — apply via Seller Central, limited to eligible ASINs
  • 7-Day Deals: Extended deal visibility on the deals page — better for new launches
  • Coupons: Percentage or dollar-off shown in search results with a green badge — low cost, high visibility
  • Subscribe & Save: 5–15% discount for repeat orders — builds recurring revenue
  • Prime Exclusive Discounts: Prime member-only pricing — boosts Prime Day and everyday visibility
  • Rollback: Temporary price reduction featured on Walmart.com homepage and category pages
  • Savings Spotlight: Curated deals placement with high visibility — requires buyer approval
  • Walmart+ Member Deals: Exclusive pricing for Walmart+ subscribers — growing importance
  • Clearance: Last resort — use only for end-of-life SKUs, as it signals distress to the algorithm
  • Q1 (Jan–Mar): New Year content reset, Valentine's Day (relevant cats), begin Prime Day planning
  • Q2 (Apr–Jun): Spring Black Friday on Lowe's/HD, Mother's Day, Memorial Day, Way Day (Wayfair), FBA inventory build for Prime Day
  • Q3 (Jul–Sep): Amazon Prime Day (mid-July, your #1 Amazon event), Back to School, Labor Day, Q4 inventory planning begins
  • Q4 (Oct–Dec): Halloween promos, Amazon Early Black Friday (late Oct), Black Friday/Cyber Monday all-hands, holiday gift guide submissions, free shipping deadlines
Buy Box management (Amazon)
  • ✓ Price competitively — #1 factor for 3P sellers winning the Buy Box
  • ✓ Use FBA or Seller Fulfilled Prime to improve shipping eligibility
  • ✓ Maintain seller feedback score above 95%
  • ✓ Keep ODR under 1% — fastest way to lose Buy Box eligibility
  • ✓ Monitor for unauthorized sellers daily using SellerApp or manual checks

Operations

The unglamorous work that keeps everything running — compliance, inventory, chargebacks, and daily monitoring.

Chargeback prevention

Amazon vendor chargebacks

  • PO compliance: ship on-time, use correct GS1-128 carton labels
  • Submit ASN (Advanced Ship Notice) before delivery — avoid PO Accuracy chargebacks
  • Follow prep compliance: poly-bagging, bubble wrap, and label placement per Amazon specs
  • Dispute chargebacks within 30 days via Vendor Central — dispute win rates can reach 60-70% with proper documentation

Retail EDI compliance

  • Walmart, Lowe's, and Home Depot require EDI 850/856/810 transactions
  • On-time fill rate targets are typically 95%+ or chargebacks apply automatically
  • Wayfair: direct-ship model — ship within 1 business day or lose Supplier Score points
  • TSC: review routing guide compliance for collect vs prepaid freight on every PO
Amazon FBA inventory health
  • ✓ IPI (Inventory Performance Index) above 400 to avoid restock limits
  • ✓ Review aged inventory monthly — 181–270 day units incur long-term storage fees
  • ✓ Use FBA removal orders for slow movers before storage fee charge dates
  • ✓ Set reorder points using "Restock Inventory" tool + your own sales velocity data
  • ✓ Spread inbound shipments across multiple FC locations when possible to reduce restock limits
